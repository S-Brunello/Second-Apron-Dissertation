# NBA Second Apron Dissertation

Data, code, and analysis for my EC4499 Economics dissertation

## Research Question

**How the NBA's second salary-cap apron reshaped demand for cost-controlled labour and roster continuity**

## Empirical Strategy

The project examines the effects of the NBA's second salary-cap apron at two levels:

1. **Player level:** Whether teams with greater pre-CBA payroll exposure increased their utilization of cost-controlled labour following the introduction of the second apron.

2. **Team level:** Whether teams with greater pre-CBA payroll exposure experienced reductions in roster continuity.

Treatment exposure is based on team payroll in the 2022-23 season, prior to ratification of the 2023 Collective Bargaining Agreement.

## Sample

2018-19 through 2025-26 NBA seasons, subject to consistent data availability.
